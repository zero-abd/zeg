Today

The first screen is the weak link.

Résumés are guesses
Keywords. Not whether they can actually do it.
Every screener is different
Different engineer, different bar, same job.
Good people slip away
The best candidates stop waiting and take the other offer.

What we built

zeg talks to every candidate — then tells you who is worth your time.

And it shows you the quotes that made it say so.

How it works

Three steps.

Step 1
They click a link
No app, no install, no scheduling back and forth.
Step 2
A 15-minute call
A real conversation. They can interrupt it.
Step 3
You get a report
A score, and the quotes behind every part of it.

Same questions, same clock, same bar — for everyone who applies.
